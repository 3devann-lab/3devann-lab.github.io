What If Shorts

What If Shorts is a personal, non-commercial tool that creates short animated "What if…?" explainer videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

It is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups or services to the public.
