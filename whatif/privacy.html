Privacy Policy

This policy explains how What If Shorts ("the app") handles information. The app is a personal tool operated by its owner for their own YouTube channel.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to manage the owner's YouTube account (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). It uses this permission only to:

  • upload videos the app created to the owner's channel,
  • set titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails on those videos, and
  • read the status of those videos (for example, whether processing finished).

The app does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google service, and it does not access other people's YouTube accounts or data.

How information is stored

The only stored credential is the Google OAuth token needed to upload, kept in encrypted form in a private location controlled by the owner. No personal information about viewers or other users is collected or stored.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube as needed to upload and manage the owner's videos.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access (security settings). After revocation the stored token stops working, and it can be deleted on request.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: contact the owner via github.com/3devann-lab.
